Good to know

Are the products original?

Yes. Every product we sell is 100% original, sealed and within its shelf life. If anything ever doesn't look right, we replace it.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 4pm usually arrive within 1 to 3 working days. Delivery is free on orders over $50, otherwise $4.

How do I pay?

Pay cash or by card when your order arrives. No card details are needed to place the order.

Can I return something?

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days. For hygiene reasons, opened lip and eye products can't be returned unless they arrived damaged.
